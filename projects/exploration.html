04 — REFLECTION

This project was another green flag that led me to the path of pursuing research. Searching through various literature and resources, including those provided by my Discrete professor, to understand a concept not taught in class was exciting. I had the freedom to scope out the project in a way that left room for complexity for rich learning while also scaling deliverables appropriately for the time I had. Having full creative control in this way made me even more motivated to learn. Additionally, having to explain concepts to others with no prior knowledge taught me how to frame my own thoughts in a legible way.

Since this project only explored symmetrical BIBDs, I would test the performance of asymmetrical BIBDs in the future. I would also add a bad actor to determine if detection and exclusion of the bad actor is possible.
