04 — REFLECTION

Looking back, my lab technique was not perfect. Our workstation was not as clean as it should've been, and we often touched other surfaces, our skin, and our clothing with our gloves. We also did not stir our solutions to make sure they were homogeneous. Lastly, our procedure was not agreed upon before we started, so measuring equipment was not chosen appropriately. A graduated cylinder could've been used instead of disposable pippetes for greater accuracy. None of these slips greatly affected the experiment, but for more complex work that is sensitive or requires sterility, results would not be trustworthy whatsoever. Therefore, I'd like to practice my lab skills in future experiments, instilling good habits over time.
